# Business Insights

## Objective

Analyze customer behavior using the Olist e-commerce dataset to understand customer distribution and repeat purchasing patterns.

---

## Insight 1: Customer Base

The dataset contains approximately 99,441 unique customers, indicating a large and diverse customer base.

**Business Impact:**
A large customer base provides opportunities for customer segmentation and targeted marketing campaigns.

---

## Insight 2: Customer Distribution

Customers are concentrated in a few states and major cities.

**Business Impact:**
The company can prioritize marketing and logistics improvements in high-performing regions while exploring strategies to grow in underrepresented areas.

---

## Insight 3: Repeat Customers

The repeat customer analysis shows that only a portion of customers make multiple purchases.

**Business Impact:**
Improving customer retention through loyalty programs, personalized recommendations, or targeted promotions could increase repeat purchases and overall revenue.

---

## Conclusion

This analysis provides a foundation for understanding customer behavior and identifying opportunities to improve customer retention and business growth.
