Product Analysis Insights
Executive Summary

This analysis evaluated product performance across the Olist e-commerce platform using sales, product, and customer review data. The goal was to identify the highest-performing product categories, understand customer satisfaction, and highlight opportunities to improve revenue and operational efficiency.

Key Insights
1. Revenue by Product Category
A small number of product categories contributed a significant share of total revenue.
These categories represent the platform's primary revenue drivers and should remain a focus for inventory planning and marketing efforts.
2. Best-Selling Categories
The highest-selling product categories consistently generated the largest order volumes.
High sales volume indicates strong customer demand and purchasing frequency.
3. Top Revenue-Generating Products
A limited number of individual products produced a disproportionately high share of total revenue.
These products should be prioritized for inventory availability and promotional campaigns.
4. Average Selling Price
Premium product categories achieved higher average selling prices than others.
These categories contribute greater revenue per transaction despite lower sales volumes.
5. Customer Satisfaction
Most product categories maintained strong average review scores, indicating positive customer experiences.
High customer ratings suggest consistent product quality and reliable fulfillment.
6. Customer Reviews
Categories with the highest sales also attracted the largest number of customer reviews.
A higher review count provides greater confidence in customer satisfaction trends.
7. High Revenue, Low Ratings
A few products generated strong revenue while receiving below-average review scores.
These products should be investigated for recurring quality, packaging, shipping, or customer expectation issues.
8. Freight Cost Analysis
Certain product categories incurred significantly higher shipping costs due to size, weight, or delivery complexity.
Optimizing shipping methods or packaging for these categories could improve overall profitability.
Business Recommendations
Continue investing in the highest-performing product categories.
Maintain adequate inventory for top-selling products to prevent stockouts.
Investigate products with low customer ratings despite strong sales.
Improve shipping efficiency for categories with high freight costs.
Encourage customer reviews to increase buyer confidence and generate additional product insights.
Use customer feedback to improve product quality and enhance the shopping experience.
Conclusion

The analysis highlights the relationship between product performance, customer satisfaction, and shipping costs. By focusing on top-performing categories, addressing low-rated products, and optimizing logistics, Olist can improve customer experience, increase profitability, and support long-term business growth through data-driven decision-making.
