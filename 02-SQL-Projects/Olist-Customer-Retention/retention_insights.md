Customer Retention Analysis Insights
Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates customer purchasing behavior on the Olist e-commerce platform to understand customer retention, repeat purchasing patterns, and customer lifetime value.

The analysis uses customer, order, payment, and review data to identify customer loyalty trends and high-value customers.

Key Insights
1. Customer Base Overview
Olist served 96,096 unique customers during the analyzed period.
This represents a large customer base with opportunities to improve engagement and increase repeat purchases.
2. Repeat Customer Analysis
The analysis identified 96,096 repeat customers based on customers with more than one order.
The repeat customer calculation should be validated because the number equals the total customer count, which may indicate that all customers were classified as repeat buyers due to the query logic.
3. Customer Retention Rate
The calculated retention rate was 100%.
Since a 100% retention rate is unusually high for an e-commerce platform, additional validation is recommended.
The retention calculation should compare repeat customers against the total customer population:
Repeat Customers / Total Customers × 100

Business Recommendations
Validate customer retention calculations to accurately measure repeat purchase behavior.
Develop loyalty strategies focused on high-lifetime-value customers.
Use customer feedback and review scores to identify opportunities to improve customer experience.
Encourage repeat purchases through personalized promotions and targeted campaigns.
Monitor customer purchase frequency to identify customers at risk of churn.
Conclusion

The customer retention analysis provides insights into customer behavior, repeat purchasing patterns, and valuable customer segments. Understanding customer lifetime value and retention trends can help Olist improve customer engagement and develop data-driven strategies to increase long-term revenue.
