Customer Retention Analysis Insights
Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates customer purchasing behavior on the Olist e-commerce platform to understand customer loyalty, repeat purchasing patterns, and customer lifetime value.

The analysis combines customer, order, payment, and review data to identify retention trends and valuable customer segments.

Key Insights
1. Customer Base Overview
Olist served 96,096 unique customers during the analyzed period.
The customer base represents a large opportunity for improving customer engagement and increasing repeat purchases.
2. Repeat Customer Behavior
Customer order frequency analysis was performed to separate one-time buyers from returning customers.
Customers with more than one completed order were classified as repeat customers.
This segmentation helps identify loyal customers and understand purchasing behavior.
3. Customer Retention Rate
The retention rate was calculated using:
Repeat Customers / Total Customers × 100
The corrected retention calculation provides a more realistic view of customer loyalty compared with the initial calculation.
Understanding retention helps businesses measure customer satisfaction and evaluate the effectiveness of loyalty strategies.

Review scores were analyzed to understand the relationship between customer satisfaction and purchasing behavior.
Customer feedback can help identify opportunities to improve products, delivery experience, and long-term retention.
Business Recommendations
Create loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.
Focus retention campaigns on customers with high lifetime value.
Use customer feedback to improve product and delivery experiences.
Monitor one-time customers and develop strategies to encourage second purchases.
Personalize marketing campaigns based on customer purchasing history.
Conclusion

The customer retention analysis provides insights into customer behavior, loyalty patterns, and valuable customer segments. By improving retention strategies and focusing on high-value customers, businesses can increase customer lifetime value and build stronger long-term relationships.
